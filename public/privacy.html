Privacy Policy

ScrapScout · Effective July 2026

The short version: you point your camera at junk, we send that one photo to an AI to price it, and then it's gone. Your inventory, your pins, your plan, and your chat live on your phone. We don't run ads, we don't sell data, and we can't see your card number.

That's the whole story. The details below just say it slower.

What leaves your phone, and why

DataWhenWhere it goes
A photo Only when you tap SCAN IT or pick from gallery Encrypted to our server, passed to Anthropic's AI to identify and value the item. We don't store it — it answers your question and it's gone from our systems.
Chat messages When you ask the scout something Sent to our server and Anthropic's AI to answer. Your conversation history is stored on your phone only, and you can clear it anytime.
Your coordinates Only when you tap MY LOCATION (or search a city) Sent directly to OpenStreetMap services (Overpass, Nominatim) to find scrap yards and places near you. Never sent to our server, never stored by us. Typing a city instead of using GPS works fully — location permission is always optional.
An item name Only when you tap "Check real eBay asks" Sent to eBay's public search API to fetch live listings. Just the item words — nothing about you.
An anonymous device ID Created on first launch A random ID (nothing about you or your phone) used to check whether this device has a Pro subscription. Stored with your subscription record at Stripe.
Your email Only if you use "Restore my Pro" Used once to find your subscription at Stripe and reconnect it. We keep no copy — Stripe already has it from your own checkout.

What never leaves your phone: your inventory and every dollar you log, your map pins, your plan and reminders, your yard notes and logged prices, and your chat history. We can't see any of it. Deleting the app deletes it.

What we don't do

  • No ads and no advertising trackers.
  • No selling or renting data. Not to anyone, not ever.
  • No analytics SDKs tracking what you do in the app.
  • No background location. GPS is read only in the moment you ask, only to search near you.
  • No accounts, no passwords. There's nothing to sign up for.

About the AI

Item identification, values, guides, listings, and chat answers are generated by Claude (Anthropic) under commercial API terms that don't use this data to train models; Anthropic may retain API data briefly for abuse monitoring before deletion — see Anthropic's privacy policy. Values are estimates, not appraisals, and the app says so wherever they appear. eBay figures shown are live asking prices from eBay's public API — the app never invents market data.

One honest caution that's also in the app: AI estimates and guidance can be wrong. Use your judgment, especially around anything electrical, fuel-powered, or sharp — and around the law guide, which is orientation, not legal advice.

Payments

ScrapScout Pro payments are handled entirely by Stripe, on Stripe's own checkout page in your browser. Your card number never touches this app or our servers. Cancel anytime from "Manage / cancel subscription" in the app.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstalling permanently deletes your inventory, pins, plan, notes, chat, and device ID.
  • At Stripe: if you subscribed and want billing records deleted, email us from your checkout email — subject to records we must legally keep (like tax records of payments).
  • Photos and chats: nothing to delete — we don't store them.

Security

Everything the app sends travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our AI, eBay, and billing keys live on our servers, never inside the app.

Children

ScrapScout is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes in any way that matters, we'll update this page and the date above. We won't quietly get worse.
